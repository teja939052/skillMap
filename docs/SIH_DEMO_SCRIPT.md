# Skill Map — SIH Demo Script

**For:** Demo video + live presentation at SIH Grand Finale  
**Duration:** 2-3 minutes for video, 5-7 minutes for live demo  
**Golden rule:** Show the product working. Do not just talk about it.

---

## Opening (10 seconds)

> "Hi, we're [Team Name] from [College Name]. We built Skill Map — a platform that connects students, colleges, and companies for skill mapping, internships, and placements."

---

## The Problem (20 seconds)

> "Right now, everyone is disconnected. Rahul, a final-year CSE student, wants a software job — but he doesn't know which skills companies actually want, which ones he already has, and which ones he's missing. Meanwhile, a company that needs 20 backend interns has no easy way to find students with the right skills. And the college has no idea what training to provide."

---

## The Demo (90 seconds) — THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART

### Step 1: Student perspective (20s)

> "Let me show you how it works. First, Rahul signs up as a student. He enters what he knows — Python, SQL, React — and what he wants — a backend developer role. He takes a quick skill assessment."

**Show:** Student dashboard → skill profile → assessment → gap analysis  
**Say:** "Skill Map shows Rahul: you're good at Python and SQL, but you need to learn Docker and REST APIs. Here are the exact courses and projects to close those gaps."

### Step 2: Company perspective (25s)

> "Now, a company posts a backend internship. They enter the required skills: Python, SQL, Docker, REST APIs."

**Show:** Company dashboard → post opportunity → enter required skills  
**Say:** "Skill Map instantly scans all registered students and finds the best matches. Rahul scores 91% — he has 3 out of 4 skills. Priya scores 87%, Arjun 82%. The company can see exactly which skills each student has and which are missing."

### Step 3: College perspective (25s)

> "Now the college principal logs in. She sees that across 500 CSE students, only 31% know Docker — but 78% of companies are asking for it."

**Show:** College dashboard → skill analytics → gap chart → create intervention  
**Say:** "The college creates a Docker workshop. 100 students complete it. Before: Docker knowledge was 32%. After: 76%. That means 47 more students now qualify for that internship. The company sees those 47 newly-qualified students immediately."

### Step 4: Faculty perspective (20s)

> "Finally, a professor who wants industry exposure sees a company has posted a real-world AI project. He brings it to his students. They build the project with industry guidance."

**Show:** Faculty dashboard → browse opportunities → collaboration module  
**Say:** "Faculty can find projects, training, and research collaboration — all in one place."

---

## Closing (10 seconds)

> "Skill Map creates a continuous loop: companies tell us what they need, colleges see the gaps, students learn the right skills, and companies find qualified candidates. That's the future of academia-industry collaboration. Thank you."

---

## What the Judges Will Ask (Prepare Answers)

| Question | Your Answer |
|----------|-------------|
| "How is this different from LinkedIn or Naukri?" | LinkedIn is a resume database. Skill Map shows *verified skills* and *skill gaps*, not just work history. Colleges see the big picture and can act on it. |
| "How do you verify skills?" | Students take tests. Faculty verify projects and certificates. Companies see verified data, not just claims. |
| "How does the matching work?" | Simple: company says "we need X, Y, Z skills". System finds students with those skills, ranks them by match percentage, shows exactly why. |
| "Is this scalable?" | Yes — API-first architecture, MongoDB for data, Redis for real-time features. Ready for multi-college deployment. |
| "What about data privacy?" | Role-based access. Students control their own data. Colleges see only aggregate analytics unless student consents. |
| "What's your business model?" | For SIH, we're showing the proof of concept. In production: freemium for students, subscription for colleges, pay-per-post for companies. |

## Slide 6 (Impact) — What to Say

> "In one college with 500 CSE students, we identified that Docker was the biggest gap — only 32% had it. After running one workshop through Skill Map, that jumped to 76%. That's 150+ students who are now job-ready for backend roles that require Docker. Multiply that across 100 colleges and 50,000 students — that's the impact."

---

## Things NOT to Say

❌ "We have an advanced competency graph with evidence provenance..."  
❌ "Our system uses a state machine for intervention outcomes..."  
❌ "We have microservices with event-driven architecture..."  

✅ "Students see what skills to learn."  
✅ "Colleges see what training to run."  
✅ "Companies find the right candidates in minutes."  
✅ "The loop continues — skills improve, more students get placed."

---

## Recording Tips for Demo Video

1. **Screen record the actual working app** — don't use slides for the demo part
2. **Use your real face/voice** — no AI narration (SIH rule)
3. **Keep it under 3 minutes**
4. **Show the full loop** in one continuous flow: company posts → college sees → training happens → students improve → company gets more candidates
5. **End with impact numbers** — judges remember numbers
